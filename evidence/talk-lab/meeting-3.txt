=== House meeting: Reeve convenes Aldric, Eliza, Bram, Corvin (seed 3) ===
  - Aldric [lord]: Aldric holds the manor's leases. Their purse strings are knotted tight. A cough's had them these two weeks.
  - Eliza [laborer]: Eliza works the fields for a day's wage. Their temper's shorter than a winter's day.
  - Bram [clergy]: Bram serves the village church. They know every soul and every soul's business. Last night's cup is still on them.
  - Corvin [clergy]: Corvin serves the village church. Their purse strings are knotted tight. They bow to no one. A cough's had them these two weeks.
  #0 Corvin [Greet]: God keep this house and all who speak plain in it.
  #1 Reeve [Question]: What ails the village most, friends? Speak true.
  #2 Eliza [Remark] (re #1): Three pennies for a week of reaping is daylight robbery.
  #3 Bram [Remark] (re #1): The common pasture is overgrazed; we must stint the number of sheep.
  #4 Aldric [PushBack] (re #2): We have argued this before and accomplished nothing.
  #5 Bram [PushBack] (re #2): Talk is cheap - who will lift a hand to do aught about it?
  #6 Eliza [PushBack] (re #3): And what wouldst thou have the village do, Bram?
  #7 Aldric [Remark] (re #6): The bailiff's cattle are eating the sweet grass reserved for the poor.
  #8 Bram [Remark] (re #7): The ditch enclosing the common wood has been unlawfully fenced off.
  #9 Corvin [Remark] (re #6): If we lose the commons, our way of life is broken forever.
  #10 Bram [Farewell]: Go in peace - and keep what was said here.
