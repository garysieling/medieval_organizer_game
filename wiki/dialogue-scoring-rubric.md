# Dialogue Scoring Rubric for AI Evaluation

**Purpose:** Standardized evaluation framework for an AI judge to score procedurally synthesized dialogue and conversation trees. Evaluates the fidelity of the specificity cascade, personality biasing, discourse acts, and social dynamics.

---

## Evaluation Categories (100 Points Total)

### 1. Topical Coherence & Discourse Flow (25 Points)
- **Act Compliance (10 pts):** Each line's discourse act correctly follows from the preceding act:
  - `Greet` is answered with `Greet`, a cautious `Remark`, or a curt `Farewell`.
  - `Question` is answered with `Answer`.
  - `Remark` is acknowledged with `Agree`, contested with `PushBack`, or elaborated upon.
- **Topical Continuity (10 pts):** Conversation maintains continuity across turn pairs without erratic topic jumping unless an intentional transition occurs.
- **Natural Cadence (5 pts):** Pacing feels conversational (neither abruptly clipped nor unnaturally verbose).

### 2. Character & Backstory Alignment (25 Points)
- **Status Grounding (10 pts):** Characters experiencing acute conditions (e.g., sickness, bereavement, hunger) reflect their physical/emotional state in speech.
- **Vocation & Ideology Voice (8 pts):** Clergy speak with pastoral cadence and blessings; lords reference leases/manor affairs; merchants speak of roads and trade; laborers speak of wages, hunger, and toil.
- **Trait Flavor & Expressiveness (7 pts):** Personality traits (Hotheaded, Kind, Proud, Pious, Gregarious, Stingy) visibly color responses (e.g. hotheads snap; the pious invoke prayers).

### 3. Specificity Cascade & Fallback Mechanism (20 Points)
- **Maximum Specificity Leading (10 pts):** When circumstantial facts match (e.g., speaking to a rival or someone who is grieving), the high-specificity rule tree initiates rather than the generic fallback.
- **Graceful Fall-Through (10 pts):** When specific dialogue topics conclude, the conversation seamlessly degrades into generic smalltalk rather than ending unnaturally.

### 4. Exclusivity & Repetition Prevention (15 Points)
- **Quip Exclusivity (10 pts):** No duplicate line templates appear within the same conversation session.
- **Material Exhaustion (5 pts):** When a topic node has exhausted fresh lines, the exchange gracefully transitions or terminates with a natural departure (`Farewell`, `WalksOff`, or `Exhausted`).

### 5. Social Dynamics & Group Meeting Threading (15 Points)
- **Relational Tone (8 pts):** Relationship status (Rival, Stranger, Kin, Friend) appropriately shifts tone (e.g., curt dismissals or fiery hostility for rivals; warmth for kin and friends).
- **Multi-Party Addressing (7 pts):** In group/house meetings, attendees dynamically bind replies to either:
  1. The organizer's open agenda question,
  2. The immediate prior speaker, or
  3. An earlier statement sharing relevant concept tags.

---

## Scoring Scale & Benchmark Grades

| Total Score | Grade | Determination | Action Required |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **90 - 100** | **A** | **Exceptional:** Vivid characterization, flawless act sequence, organic fallback. | Production ready. |
| **80 - 89** | **B** | **Good:** Solid coherence, noticeable personality, minor stylistic repetition. | Acceptable for merge. |
| **70 - 79** | **C** | **Adequate:** Functional but occasionally bland or mildly mismatched tone. | Minor prompt/weight tuning. |
| **< 70** | **D / F** | **Deficient:** Non-sequiturs, rule violations, ignored backstories, looping. | Reject; debug selection logic. |
