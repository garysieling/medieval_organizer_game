## The book

Richard Williams' "The Animator's Survival Kit" (2001) — the definitive animation-principles text. Written for hand-drawn character animation but its principles are about *movement and weight* — they apply directly to sprite/character animation in pixel art.

## The core principles (adapted to pixel isometric)

### The 12 principles of animation (Disney lineage, distilled)

| Principle | What it means | Pixel-art application |
|---|---|---|
| **Squash & stretch** | volume shifts with motion | sprite deformation on impact, walk-bob |
| **Anticipation** | a wind-up before the action | a gather before a jump, a lean before a step |
| **Staging** | the action reads clearly | silhouette clarity — the pose must read at 16-32px |
| **Follow-through & overlap** | parts lag behind the core | clothing, tools, hair trail the body |
| **Slow in / slow out** | acceleration curves, not linear | easing on walk cycles, door swings |
| **Arcs** | natural motion is curved | tools swing on arcs, limbs pendulum |
| **Secondary action** | a gesture adds to the main action | a baker wiping hands, a worker adjusting grip |
| **Timing** | frames = weight + personality | a heavy stomp vs a quick step is *timing* |
| **Exaggeration** | push it past real until it reads | at pixel scale, exaggeration is *required* for readability |
| **Solid drawing** | weight, balance, volume | the isometric body must *sit* on its feet |
| **Appeal** | characters should be interesting to look at | the villager-sprite personality spec |

### Walk cycles (the backbone)

- Williams' contact-down-passing-up breakdown is the universal walk model — even a 4-frame pixel walk obeys the same pose logic
- Different villagers walk differently: a heavy stomp (elder, laborer), a quick step (youth), a shuffle (tired), a march (soldier) — walk *is* character

## What it buys game2

- **Character through motion** — the psych/traits systems surface in animation: a proud villager's upright gait, a beaten-down one's shuffle — motion *is* the personality
- **Weight and presence** — the difference between "a sprite that moves" and "a person walking" is weight/timing — Williams is the manual for that
- **Readability at small scale** — exaggeration + staging are the tools for making actions legible at pixel scale
