## Plan — Sound A5 (#1501): nine sim cues + palette

`SfxCue.kind` is `&'static str`; the audio side already has `CueKind`/`SynthKind` for the 8 legacy cues. Emit sites from the census:

### game2-core

- `alarm`: ChimneyFire lit (`ill_luck.rs`), lightning strike lit (`sky.rs`), crackdown arm of `march_resolved` (`verbs.rs`)
- `toll`: per `newly_dead` in `grief_tick` (`people.rs`); funeral column beats in `formation_step`, capped 1 per 120 frames (new `funeral_toll_frame` field)
- `cheer`: `march_resolved` non-crackdown resolution; `townhall_at` Carries arm
- `chant`: `formation_step` while `FormationKind::March` is live, once per sim day (new `chant_day` field)
- `coins`: `arrive` BuyFrom arm when `paid.is_some()`
- `quill`: `CtxAct::Petition` each press
- `door`: `survey_tick` canvass knock at the door tile
- `cart_arrive`: `arrive` on Cart/Levy/LevyCart/Deliver/Remit arrivals
- `gate`: `advance_travels` tile-transition onto a gate tile (new `TileMap::is_gate` accessor; edge-triggered, standing doesn't refire)
- `docs/protocol.md`: new kinds listed

### game2-audio

- `synth.rs`: 9 `SynthKind` variants with recipes (alarm=fast tolling, toll=slow bell, cheer=noise swell, chant=formant pad, coins=metallic ticks, quill=scratch, door=knock, cart=clatter, gate=creak)
- `palette.rs`: `voice_for(CueKind) -> VoiceSpec { sample: Option<&'static str>, synth: SynthKind, bus, gain, pan_from_pos }`, exhaustive match; host resolves a ledgered sample when the bank has it, synth otherwise (ledger is empty today — everything resolves synth until A4)
- `host.rs`: new `CueKind` variants, `from_name`, render routes through the palette

### Tests

- `session/tests/sfx.rs`: the 10 named tests (`alarm_on_fire_start`, `alarm_on_crackdown`, `toll_on_death`, `cheer_on_townhall_win`, `chant_once_per_day_while_march_live`, `coins_on_purchase`, `quill_on_petition`, `door_on_visit`, `cart_arrive_on_delivery`, `gate_edge_triggered_once`)
- `game2-audio`: `palette_covers_every_cue`; new voices in the non-silent/no-NaN render test
