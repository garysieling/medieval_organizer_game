Sound A4: ambience mixer, first asset batch, four place scenarios.

## Summary

- `soundscape/ambience.rs` (new): `Ambience::update(emitters, seed, frame, engine)` — one looping voice per bed kind with ~2 s eased gain toward `weight*gain*BED_LEVEL`, distance lowpass + reverb send from the emitter; Poisson one-shots at `rate_hz`/`min_gap_s` from `Rng::new(seed ^ frame_hash)`, round-robin variants, ±3% pitch / ±2 dB gain, max 2 voices per emitter kind; fresh one-shots duck beds 2 dB (500 ms release). SnowHush/Haar cap the master lowpass / raise the reverb floor.
- `engine.rs`: `Source::SynthBed` (own-synth wind/rain beds, plays until `stop`), `set_params`/`alive` for eased retunes, a 5 ms release fade on non-looping samples (they used to truncate mid-waveform = clicks), band-limited noise for the synth beds (`lp2` integrator — raw white draws read as click storms), limiter ceiling −1 dBFS → −1.5 dBFS so oversampled true-peak stays under −1 dBTP.
- `host.rs`: `AudioFrame` gains `emitters` (+ `from_model_with(model, listener)` for a real ear); the host owns an `Ambience` and updates it once per pushed frame in `render_block`; `set_bank` hands a `SampleBank` to the engine (live hosts pass it to the device half via `pending_bank`).
- `synth.rs`: `BedKind::{Wind,Rain}` stationary noise beds + `smooth()` band-limit constants.
- `scenarios.rs`: `Op::Look(x,y)`/`LookMarket`/`LookSkeps`/`LookChurch`/`LookGate` + `look_spot`, and the four place scenarios: `apiary_spring`, `holy_day_church`, `market_square`, `winter_gate`.
- `sound_dump.rs`: `Look*` ops steer the listener; the dump loads `assets/sound` like `game_ui` does.
- `animals.rs`: periodic rain/anvil/festival cue emissions removed from `sfx_tick` (the place-based scan owns the bed); bell/wonder/snowballs/splash stay.
- Assets: 6 of 17 shipped — Kenney CC0 (`church_bell`, `anvil_hit`, `door_creak`, `gate_creak`, `cart_wheel`, `hoofbeat`), loudnorm'd + ledgered via `sound_process.py`. The 7 beds and 4 animal shots need Freesound; `FREESOUND_API_KEY` was unset, so they follow up in **#1575 (A4b)** with the two `batch_*_shipped` tests `#[ignore]`d meanwhile.
