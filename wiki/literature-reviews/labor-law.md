# Comparative Labor Law Literature Review

**Survey Date:** 2026-11-14
**Issue:** #1212
**Agent:** Literature Survey Agent
**Confidence:** High
**Domain:** Labor law — US/France/Spain today, medieval anchors

## Summary

Labor law across systems converges on five institutional shapes worth stealing: **the combination ban** (when talking about wages is itself the crime), **the standing document** (the paper that proves where you belong), **the peer tribunal** (tradesfolk judging tradesfolk), **the protected ask** (a grievance in the right form can't be punished), and **the in-kind leak** (wage caps don't work because pay finds other channels). The medieval anchors aren't decoration — the Statute of Labourers is literally the game's antagonist technology, and the journeyman combinations it tried to crush are the org's ancestors.

## Key Sources

### Ordinance & Statute of Labourers (England, 1349/1351)
**Rating:** A+ — primary medieval anchor, *this is the game's period*
**Relevance:** The lord's legal toolkit
**Key Findings:**
- Post-plague labor scarcity doubled wages; the crown's answer was a wage cap at pre-plague rates, compulsory work for the able-bodied, and combination-for-higher-pay treated as conspiracy — the cause born criminal or not at all.
- It *failed* as price control because employers leaked pay through the in-kind channel (board, drink, extras, remitted dues) — law shaped how pay moved, not whether.

### NLRA/Wagner Act (US, 1935) & Taft-Hartley (1947)
**Rating:** A — statutory framework, extensive case law
**Relevance:** The protection shape — what's protected and what isn't
**Key Findings:**
- Protected **concerted activity**: the protection attaches to acting *together*, and punishing the asking is itself the unfair practice — the legal shield follows the *form* of the grievance.
- Counter-moves are also codified: what the power-holder may not do (retaliate, spy, promise) — the boss's playbook is enumerated, which is what makes inoculation (#1246) teachable.

### France — code du travail / prud'hommes / livret ouvrier
**Rating:** A — statutory + institutional history
**Relevance:** The peer tribunal and the standing document
**Key Findings:**
- **Prud'hommes**: labor courts of elected employers *and* workers judging together — peer judgment legitimizes verdicts the state couldn't impose; peers judge by craft custom and close ranks against outsiders.
- **Livret ouvrier** (to 1890): the worker's book recording employment/character; unmarked meant unemployable — the document *is* the leash, so controlling/forging the document is real power.

### Spain — fueros, comisiones obreras
**Rating:** B+ — institutional history
**Relevance:** Customary law and the co-opted council
**Key Findings:**
- Municipal **fueros** layered local custom over royal law — the petition/custom protection shape.
- **Comisiones obreras**: state works councils built to co-opt labor were infiltrated and turned from within — the power-holder's own council becoming the organizing vehicle (informative for our seats/infiltration play, not filed as a separate gap).

### Craft guild ordinances & journeyman combinations
**Rating:** A− — secondary but consistent (Webb, Epstein, Unwin histories)
**Relevance:** The org's literal ancestors
**Key Findings:**
- Journeyman associations struck for wages and were suppressed as conspiracies — guilds *were* the employer side; the combination below the master tier is the proto-union, which is what our org is.

## Game Mechanic Proposals

### Mechanic 1: The Statute of Labourers — the wage-cap edict → **#1260**
The antagonist's tool: posted max rates, asking-for-more itself actionable, combination-as-conspiracy. Enforcement fails in proportion to how badly hands are needed — the law's realism is its leak.

### Mechanic 2: The servant's pass → **#1261**
The livret ouvrier medievalized: a mark that proves where you belong. Masterless folk are swept up first; forging/holding/un-marking passes is org business. Pairs with #1258 (the false traveller's papers are his tell).

### Mechanic 3: The peer tribunal → **#1262**
Prud'hommes medievalized: masters and journeymen judging disputes inside a trade, by craft custom not statute. The org can pack it — lawful cover for members inside a trade.

### Mechanic 4: The right to petition → **#1263**
Concerted-activity protection medievalized: a formal petition's named signatories carry customary protection — go public early under the shield or stay covert; the lord's counter is to read the same document as conspiracy.

### Mechanic 5: Over-cap pay in kind → **#1264**
The wage cap's quiet leak: employers compete through board, drink, cloth, remitted dues — `livelihood.rs` tracks effective wage (coin + extras), and holding a master's over-pay is leverage.

### Not proposed (and why)
- **Dues checkoff, grievance arbitration, NLRB adjudication** — institutional plumbing of a formalized union; our org is pre-formal, the interesting machinery is what precedes recognition.
- **Closed shop / secondary boycotts** — needs a recognized union to mean anything; same reason.
- **Co-opted works council (comisiones)** — the seats/infiltration layer already carries the shape; not a separate gap.
