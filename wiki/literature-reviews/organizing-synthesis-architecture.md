# Synthesis: The Organizing/Power Architecture — Unified Model Across 12 Surveys

**Date:** 2026-10-31
**Agent:** Literature Survey Agent
**Type:** Synthesis doc — unifies the organizing research series into a system architecture
**Status:** Proposed architecture, not yet implemented
**Surveys synthesized:** organizing-historical-campaigns, organizing-countertactics, organizing-enforcement-and-defense, organizing-campaign-masters, organizing-modern-counter-playbook, organizing-information-infrastructure, organizing-anticolonial-campaigns, organizing-messianic-prophetic, organizing-premodern-resistance, organizing-secret-societies, organizing-faith-and-power, organizing-critical-pedagogy (+ sprint-3/4/7 theory base)

## The One-Sentence Model

**Power contests between an organized community and entrenched power are fought across three coupled systems: (1) how the movement mobilizes (org form), (2) what institutions produce what kinds of subjects (subject-production), and (3) how power strikes back (the counter-playbook) — across a lifecycle where winning is the midpoint, not the end.**

---

## System 1: The Campaign Lifecycle (the temporal spine)

```
build → mobilize → campaign → win/lose → enforce → defend (forever)
```

Each phase needs different mechanics — the single biggest correction from the research is that "win" is phase 3 of 5, not the ending:

| Phase | What happens | Doc sources | Key stats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Build** | 1-1s, house meetings, leadership pipeline, org infrastructure | sprint-3, sprint-4, campaign-masters | relationships, organic leaders, `organized_power` |
| **Mobilize** | choose org form (open/secret/prophetic/anchored); structure tests reveal strength | historical-campaigns, secret-societies, messianic | `capacity`, `perceived_power`, form choice |
| **Campaign** | escalation ladder: petition → structure test → strike/boycott → confrontation → settlement | historical-campaigns, campaign-masters | `discipline`, `logistics`, demand specificity |
| **Win/Lose** | settlement or defeat; wins trigger counter-timer | historical-campaigns | `win_integrity`, `counter_movement_timer` |
| **Enforce** | win decays unless maintained; watchdog roles, compliance monitoring | enforcement-and-defense | `win_integrity` decay vs maintenance |
| **Defend** | counter-movement (never disbands) runs long-cycle attacks: litigation, legislation, cultural reversal | enforcement-and-defense, modern-playbook | `win_integrity`, `paranoia`, solidarity |

**The rule that fell out of the cases:** movements disband after winning; losers reorganize after losing. So the enemy-side AI must treat defeat as regrouping, not elimination.

## System 2: Mobilization Modes (the org-form spectrum)

The single most consequential choice a movement makes — it determines growth speed, counter-vulnerability, legitimacy, and collapse mode:

| Form | Growth | Strength | Weakness | Historical exemplars | Counter-move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Open organizing** | Slow, compounding | Legitimacy, durability, mass scale | Visible → raid, lawfare, infiltration | IAF/COPS, UFW, unions | Infiltration, legal harassment |
| **Secret society** | Medium, capped | Raid-resistant | Infiltration is structural; isolation from legitimacy; ritual drift | FLN, IRB, Carbonari, Mau Mau | Penetration (Le Caron), counter-ritual, villagization |
| **Prophetic mobilization** | Explosive | Speed, total commitment | Deadline kill-switch; single point of failure; capture-prone | Niklashausen, Münster, Savonarola | Kill/capture prophet; run out the clock; co-option |
| **Parallel institution** | Slow, deep | Outlives repression; IS an alternative state | Low offensive capacity | Waldensians, Palmares, BECs, underground churches | Attrition, delegitimation, incorporation |
| **Institutionally anchored** | Medium, steady | Borrowed trust+venue+durability | Host institution moderates message; can withdraw cover | COPS in parishes, SCLC in churches | Attack the host; incorporation offers |
| **Crowd/spontaneous** | Instant spike | Attention | No capacity — dissolves (GLF, Jacquerie) | Riots, leaderless upsurges | Wait it out; isolate; channel into hearings |

**Design implication:** org form should be a *choice with modifiers*, and forms can hybridize (open front + clandestine wing = dual-org architecture; prophetic burst converted into structural org = the movement that survives its prophet).

## System 3: Subject-Production (the deep layer — Foucault × Freire)

The deepest contest: **institutions manufacture the dispositions that determine whether people can mobilize at all.** Both sides build subject-producing institutions:

| Power-side institutions | Produce | Movement counter-institutions | Produce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parish (as state church) | obedience, legitimation | House meetings, base communities | `critical_agency`, solidarity |
| School (as discipline) | docile citizens | Folk/citizenship/freedom schools | `organic_leaders` |
| Plantation chapel / Slave Bible | pacification | Hidden scripture networks, movement press | counter-narrative |
| Workhouse/asylum | stigmatized surplus | Mutual aid societies | `dignity` (McAlevey's core finding) |
| Company union / captive seat | performative voice | Real unions/committees | `organized_power` |

**Citizen disposition stats** (proposed): `internalized_domination` (initiative suppression; lowered by conscientization actions), `critical_agency`, `courage`, `discipline`, `dignity`. Institutions on both sides are competing to shape these. The broker class (priests, drivers, foremen, clerks) is the contested intermediary that tilts either way.

## System 4: The Counter-Playbook (power-holder AI)

The enemy toolkit, now spanning medieval → modern with documented doctrine at every level:

```
SURVEILLANCE → INFILTRATION → LEGAL/ADMIN ATTRITION → PROVOCATION → REPRESSION
                ↘  CO-OPTION TRACK ↗                ↘ PRIVATIZED MARKET ↗
```

| Track | Tools | Era-proof form |
|---|---|---|
| **Surveillance** | census, dossiers, informers, spyware | The informant becomes a subscription; examination is eternal |
| **Infiltration** | agents, provocateurs, bleuite turned operatives | Le Caron (25 yrs), Fournier's network, TigerSwan |
| **Legal attrition** | gag rules, permits, injunctions, SLAPPs, RICO, reporting burdens | Foreign-agent laws; Mohawk Valley playbook |
| **Co-option** | incorporation, licensed religion, captive seats, moderate-split settlements | Constantine, millet, company unions, reform absorption |
| **Provocation** | agents urging violence at the worst moment; false flags | Provocateurs → discipline break → legitimized repression |
| **Repression** | raids, mass arrest, violence | Deferred `radicalization_debt` (Sétif→FLN; Sharpeville→UDF) — tactical win, strategic loss |
| **Info war** | media framing, astroturf, snitch-jacket, redacted canon, zone-flooding | Slave Bible → planted stories → platform manipulation |

**Counter-countertactics (player abilities):** assume-infiltration design, relational vetting (1-1s as security), marshal discipline training, verify-don't-accuse norms, exploiting known taps for disinfo (Birmingham), distributed leadership, documentation/counter-intel (the Media PA break-in as ultimate move), independent press, solidarity networks, legal infrastructure.

## System 5: The Actor Taxonomy

Roles that recur across every documented movement:

- **Organizer** — builds, trains, doesn't govern (player starts here)
- **Organic leader** — emerges from the constituency, leads their own (the pipeline product)
- **Prophet** — mobilizes by revelation; explosive, fragile, uncapturable-hence-uncheckable
- **Broker/intermediary** — dual loyalty; filters power both ways (priest, driver, clerk, mission-schooled elite)
- **Watchdog** — post-win enforcement role (new from enforcement doc)
- **Marshal/peacekeeper** — discipline enforcement at actions (provocateur counter)
- **Canvasser/educator** — produces the leadership pipeline itself (petition canvassers → suffrage; citizenship teachers)
- **Infiltrator/provocateur** — the enemy inside (the counter-role)
- **Snitch/informer class** — made, not born (relapse mechanics, leverage)

## The Stats Model (unified across docs)

```rust
// Org-level
organized_power    // exists in organized_power.rs — capacity, grows on good use, atrophies idle
perceived_power    // Alinsky rule: what the enemy thinks you have — drives negotiate/repress thresholds
discipline         // nonviolent/organizational discipline; the jiu-jitsu asset AND the provocateur target
paranoia           // trust-damage stat; infiltration's real damage (immune overreaction)
logistics          // food/comm/legal supply lines; determines how long actions can hold
brand_saturation   // recognition × replication-cost of symbols
win_integrity      // post-win decay state; maintenance vs. drain
penetration        // accrual of enemy informer presence (clandestine orgs)

// Citizen-level (proposed)
internalized_domination  // initiative suppression; lowered only by conscientization
critical_agency          // produced by counter-institutions
courage                  // action threshold
dignity                  // McAlevey: the real workplace stake
broker_duality           // intermediary class: loyalty split stat

// Power-holder
legitimacy               // constraint on repression; spent by backfire events
repression_debt          // deferred radicalization from atrocities (arrives on delay)
perceived_threat         // their model of you (the Alinsky mirror)
```

## Implementation Path (suggested)

1. **Phase A — the loop**: extend `organized_power.rs` + `community.rs` with campaign structure (demand, escalation, settlement) and `perceived_power` — the core loop that makes organizing *playable*
2. **Phase B — the enemy**: repression tech tree + provocateur/infiltration events + paranoia — the game gets teeth
3. **Phase C — the depth**: internalized_domination/critical_agency stats + counter-institution buildings (folk school, movement press, mutual aid) — the subject-production layer
4. **Phase D — the forms**: org-form spectrum (secret cells, prophetic events, parallel institutions, dual-org architecture)
5. **Phase E — the long game**: win-decay + counter-movement timer + watchdog roles — victory becomes a phase, not an ending

## Design Flags (open questions)

- **Ethics layer**: AI power-holders should run the dirty playbook (provocateurs, infiltration, co-option) — players get counter-countertactics. If players can run repression, need a design decision on framing.
- **The prophet problem**: prophetic mobilization is the most fun and most fragile mode — do players get to *be* prophets, or only convert prophetic bursts into structure?
- **Broker play**: can players *be* intermediaries (the priest straddling both), or only convert them?
- **Where this meets medieval theming**: every mechanic has a documented medieval form (the Inquisition, Waldensians, charivari, guild/commune politics, prophetic preachers, secret confraternities) — the modern-history mechanics are themable without anachronism.
