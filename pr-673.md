## Summary

- **Churchyard burials (#673):** a village death lies in where it fell for `LYING_IN_DAYS = 3` days, then `graveyard_tick` (daily, `sim.graveyard`) carries the entity's `Pos` once to the next slot on a deterministic church-side plan and marks it `villager::Buried`. Slots fill in death order; country `Outlander`s and the martyr's plot keep the old stay-put graves.
- **Public model:** `PersonV.epitaph` ("died day N, of ...") + `PersonV.buried`, `UiModel.graveyard` slot list — all `#[serde(default)]`, documented in `docs/protocol.md`.
- **Client:** buried villagers draw the new `art.tombstones` pack (RD-generated `tomb1..3.png`, $0.24) with the flat `graves` marks kept for the lying-in; graveyard plots render as worn dirt (`graveyard_dirt`, snow-aware); hovering a grave shows name + epitaph. `GAME2_KILL=N` headless hook added for dump checks.
- **#674 seam:** the carry is the minimal burial trigger; procession, mourners and rites build on it.
