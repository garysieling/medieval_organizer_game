# Reformation mechanic (#671)

A provoked villager turns on the parish and starts something: a splinter
congregation that keeps its own word on the green.

## What changed

- **`sim::religion::Reformed`** — the faction marker: every green
  fellow wears it, and it is what holy-day routing and the splinter's
  service read. Members also carry `Faith.religion = "the green
  fellowship"`, a `"the green word"` tradition, and `Apostate` (they
  walked out of the fold).
- **Splinter congregation** — `Game::splinter: Option<Entity>` holds a
  real second `Congregation` ("the green fellowship", low orthodoxy,
  reformer as leader), created lazily at ignition. `perform_service`
  was already entity-parameterized, so the green keeps its own office:
  `service_tick` now runs a second service on the splinter with
  `charity_output: 0` — the poor box stays the parish's.
- **Sparks, not rolls** — `spark_reformation(tick, kind)` is called by
  three authored provocations: the `Heretic` rare event's word, the
  doctor iatrogenic-harm line ("the doctor's hands left him worse"),
  and the weekly tithe grievance in `exit_tick`. The first spark
  kindles a 30-day fuse; later strikes change nothing.
- **Ignition** — `reformation_tick` (daily, beside `service_tick`)
  spends a lit spark on the most susceptible soul: adult, in-village,
  `Faith`-bearing, not old-faith, not clergy, of the fold, carrying
  **two or more live grievances**. Score = grievances + thin devotion
  + weak priest bond + pious, deterministic `mix()` throughout. On
  ignition the reformer leaves the parish roll, preaches a green-word
  line, the priest denounces, and `orthodoxy >= 0.7` parishans get the
  ladder's first rung (`Gossip`) on the spot.
- **Defection** — once born, the fellowship grows: each day every
  eligible parish heart rolls a `mix()`-deterministic chance scaled by
  the same susceptibility (no grievance needed to drift). Defectors
  move parish→splinter with the same marks and a `Doctrine`
  grievance. A dead or absent leader hands the word to the next soul.
- **`Problem::Doctrine`** — "hears the plain word" / issue tag
  `"the true faith"` → issue name "the parish's crooked ways". It
  flows through the existing talk → `hear` → Organizer → campaign
  path, so the player's foment route is the one #864/#668 already
  built: hear the grievance, found the issue, stand in the gathering.
- **Holy-day routing** — `routine_dawn` partitions three ways now:
  parish flock → church rows, old faith → stones/grove (untouched),
  `Reformed` → a `Spot::Green` ring by the market cross (offset from
  the feast centre, leader at the heart when they come). Green
  fellows always answer the call — the attendance roll still runs for
  everyone else so the rng stream doesn't shift. `routine_text` says
  "preaching the green word" / "at the green word".
- **Player** — no new verbs: `CtxAct::Attend` already fires on holy
  days and counts any `Mode::Worshipping` gathering, green included.
  The player has no `Faith` component, so there is no literal "join"
  — attending and furthering "the true faith" issue is the
  participation path, per the grilling.

## Tests

`cargo test -p game2-core --lib session::tests::reformation` — 11
tests: spark→reformer ignition and marks, no-spark silence, spark
fade, follower defection growth, holy-day green-vs-church split,
splinter devotion without parish charity, orthodoxy-gated sanction,
old-faith immunity, player Attend, leader succession on death, and
same-seed determinism.

`cargo test -p game2-core --lib` — full suite incl. determinism and
layering guards: **1170 passed, 0 failed**.
`cargo test -p game2-core --test protocol_doc` — pass (no wire change).

No wire-protocol or client changes: the green gathering renders
through the existing routine/spot machinery and the inspector's
routine line; no new `UiModel`/`CtxAct` fields.
