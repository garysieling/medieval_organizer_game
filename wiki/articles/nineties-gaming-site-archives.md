# 90s Gaming Site Archives

**Type:** Source/Reference Collection
**Source:** Backlog item (Medium) — "90s gaming site archives: historical game-design content."
**Date:** 2026-10-25

## Description

90s/early-00s game design websites and community archives — a treasure of *in-the-moment* design thinking from the city-builder/strategy era this game draws from. Sources like the Impressions Games communities, Apolyton, SimCity forums, r.g.s. archives, early Gamasutra-era content.

## Why they matter to game2

- **The design lineage** — Caesar/Pharaoh/Zeus-era city-builders are our clearest ancestors; their communities documented what worked, what didn't, what players actually did with the systems
- **Era-authentic thinking** — pre-monetization, pre-engagement-metrics design thinking; games designed to be *fun* not *sticky*
- **Community norms** — how those communities discussed/solved design problems; useful precedent for our own dev-community approach

## Key sources

| Source | Value |
|---|---|
| **Apolyton Civilization Site** | the deep-design community for Civ-era games; strategy mechanics analysis at fan level |
| **HeavenGames (Caesar/Pharaoh/Zeus/AoE)** | city-builder community strategy guides, housing-block efficiency, walker mechanics |
| **Simtropolis / SimCity 4 community** | city-builder UGC culture, realism debates, region play |
| **Old GameFAQs-era walkthroughs/guides** | how players actually *used* the systems — emergent strategies the designers didn't intend |
| **Early Gamasutra/postmortems** | in-the-moment dev postmortems (M.U.L.E., SimCity, Civ) — real design reasoning |
| **rec.games.* Usenet archives** | unfiltered 90s design discussion, no polish, real arguments |

## What to extract

- **Walker/coverage mechanics** — how city-builder NPCs actually delivered services (the real engine under the city-builder feel)
- **Housing upgrade chains** — the classic city-builder core loop
- **Emergent strategies the community found** — the most instructive content is *unintended* play
- **The "cozy" recipe** — what made these games feel warm/inhabited (music, ambient detail, readable density)
- **Design-density decisions** — what level of simulation detail actually paid off vs what was ignored complexity

## Method

- Wayback Machine for dead sites; mirror what we use before it rots further
- Extract into our wiki format (mechanics to steal, pitfalls to avoid)

## Rating

A reference well — the 90s city-builder design corpus is our closest ancestor and most of it is only in these archives.
