## Summary

- **Funeral processions (#674):** when the churchyard's day comes, `graveyard_tick` calls `begin_funeral` instead of teleporting the body. The mourners form a `FormationKind::Funeral` column - the officiant leads (priest for the parish, the old faith's pious elder for the stones, the congregation leader behind them; a column with no clergy marches all the same), up to 4 pallbearers (the dead's warmest bonds, nearest neighbours in a pinch) shoulder the body, and 4-7 faithful followers trail. One column at a time; burials that can't form keep the old laid-in-where-it-lies fallback.
- **The body rides, it isn't beamed:** `villager::Funeral.carried` latches only when a bearer reaches the death tile; until then the corpse lies in place while the column walks to it, then its `Pos` sits at the lead bearers' midpoint so rear stragglers can't drag it back. `funeral_resolved` lays it in the allocated slot, warms the mourners' bonds a touch, adds a little devotion, and puts "Earth to earth." in the officiant's mouth.
- **Formation fixes the column needed:** pace now scales with `walk_pace()` (turbo no longer leaves a funeral crawling for days), the gather grace counts on `waited` separately from the per-leg stall budget (`leg0`-scaled: twice the leg's expected walk + slack), a boxed-in head slips out by whichever neighbour still closes on the target, and skipped legs land the head on the next waypoint instead of the wall it hugged. The same instant-skip bug existed for parades/marches and is fixed for all kinds.
- **Lychgate:** `plan_graveyard` picks a walkable tile by the first plot -> `Graveyard.gate` -> `UiModel.lychgate` (serde-defaulted, documented in `docs/protocol.md`); the client draws the RD-generated `lychgate1..2.png` pack as a standing piece and simply doesn't draw when the pack's absent.
- **Daily plans yield:** `routine_dawn` skips `InFormation` villagers so the day's schedule can't yank a mourner out of the column.
