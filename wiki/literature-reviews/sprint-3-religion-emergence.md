# Lit Review - Sprint 3: Religion Emergent Effects

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Domain:** Cognitive Science of Religion / Social Simulation
**Sources:** 4 works on religious simulation

## Sources

### 1. "Simulating Religion" (Cambridge, 2024)
- Whole field: computational modeling of religion as complex system
- **Finding:** Simulation reveals how fragmentary theories relate + untangles causal influence
- **Relevance:** HIGH - Validates our whole approach to religion as emergent system

### 2. "How Religious Fundamentalism Takes Root" (Cambridge-INET)
- ABM of religiosity dynamics; fundamentalism = cohesive subset with very high religiosity
- **Finding:** Fundamentalism emerges from: weaker peer attachment, higher income, less substitutability between religious/secular goods, higher prices for secular goods, lower tolerance
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - Fundamentalism can EMERGE in our sim from these conditions, not be scripted. Maps to our community/econ systems.

### 3. "Religious Diversity and Social Cohesion" (Snijders, SAOM)
- Homophily + diversity effects on cohesion; micro-macro transition
- **Finding:** Micro-level homophily produces macro-level cohesion patterns; context matters
- **Relevance:** HIGH - Homophily (like-attracts-like) drives religious community formation. Emergent from micro rules.

### 4. "Religion, Empathy, and Cooperation" (Supernatural Punishment Hypothesis)
- Religion enabled shift from small to large societies via cooperation
- **Finding:** Empathy modulated by religious identity signals affects cooperation
- **Relevance:** HIGH - Religious identity as cooperation signal; large-scale social cohesion mechanism

## Key Emergent Effects

1. **Fundamentalism emergence** - not assigned; emerges from weak peer bonds + wealth + secular goods scarcity + intolerance. Perfect for our emergent design.

2. **Club goods** - religious groups produce shared benefits for members only → incentivizes joining + commitment. Mechanic: congregations produce goods non-members can't access.

3. **Homophily** - similar agents cluster → religious communities form from micro preferences. Emergent community structure.

4. **Supernatural punishment** - belief in oversight → enables cooperation at scale → larger societies. Mechanic: faith reduces defection in social/econ interactions.

5. **Religious identity signals** - visible markers modulate cooperation. Mechanic: agents display faith; affects who cooperates with whom.

## Mechanics to Extract

- **Fundamentalism conditions**: weak peer attachment + income + secular scarcity + intolerance → extremist community emerges
- **Club goods**: congregation benefits (cohesion, charity, identity) only for members → drives joining + orthodoxy
- **Homophily clustering**: agents prefer similar → spontaneous religious communities
- **Cooperation scaling**: faith signals enable cooperation beyond kin → larger settlements
- **Orthodoxy enforcement**: high-devotion members police norms → pressure to conform

## Design Proposals

- Religious identity as visible trait affecting cooperation
- Congregation club goods (exclusive benefits)
- Homophily-based community formation
- Fundamentalism emerges under specific conditions (weak bonds + scarcity + intolerance)

## Rating
**Critical** - Sources show religion's emergent effects (fundamentalism, cohesion, cooperation scaling) can all emerge from simple rules. Directly models what games haven't captured.
