# Regional Architectural Variety

**Type:** Visual Reference / World Design
**Source:** Backlog item (Medium, artistic) — "Regional architectural variety."
**Date:** 2026-10-25

## Description

How buildings differ across regions — the visual expression of `regional-ruler-personality.md`. Architecture should make *where you are* legible at a glance: a Mediterranean hilltown doesn't look like a Northern timber village.

## Regional vocabularies

| Region | Palette | Roofs | Walls | Telltale elements |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Northern/Timber** | browns, moss greens, slate | steep pitched, thatch/shingle | timber-frame, wattle-daub, stone base | tall chimneys, wooden market halls |
| **Mediterranean** | ochres, whites, terracotta | low pitch, tile | plastered stone, stucco | arches, courtyards, shutters, bell towers |
| **Hanseatic/Trade** | brick reds, dark timber | tall gabled, stepped | brick + timber | warehouses, crane-houses, guild halls |
| **Highland/Frontier** | grey stone, heather | stone-slab, turf | rough fieldstone | brochs, fortified farmhouses, keeps |
| **Desert/Oasis fringe** | sand, white, indigo | flat domed | mudbrick, adobe | arcades, wind-towers, water channels |

## The generation layer

- Region template from world gen (`world-generation-exploration.md`) assigns an architectural vocabulary: material palette + roof forms + detail kit
- Buildings are *compositional* — a "bakery" is a base form + regional materials + craft-specific details (oven chimney, sign); region changes the *dressing*, not the function
- Wealth shows: a rich district's materials upgrade (tile over thatch, carved over plain); poor districts degrade

## Consistency rules

- Same building *type* = same silhouette across regions (a windmill is a windmill) — readability preserved
- Materials vary but proportions hold — the style is in materials/details, not bone structure
- Within a region, consistent palette — a village shouldn't mix styles randomly

## Readability test

Zoom out to the isometric view: can you tell which region you're in without a label? If yes, the variety is working.
