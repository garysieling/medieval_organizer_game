## Summary
The Jacquarda Bastarda bake squashed its native 13px raster 20% with a
`BOX` resample under a hard threshold at 140 - thin gothic strokes
average below the cutoff and vanish, so glyphs like g, s, w and e
fragment and body prose goes unreadable at 1x.

`bake_font.py` now resamples with `LANCZOS` and thresholds at 100, which
keeps the strokes through the squash. Same 10x13 cells, same
GLYPH_W/GLYPH_H metrics - no layout changes. `glyphs.rs` regenerated.

Verified visually: a `--dump-frame 300` run shows the log, talk panel
and cards reading cleanly at 1x where the old bake broke up.
