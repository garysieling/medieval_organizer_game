=== House meeting: Reeve convenes Aldric, Eliza, Bram, Corvin (seed 27) ===
  - Aldric [clergy]: Aldric serves the village church. They're the first with a loaf when a house is bare. They'd sooner watch the clouds than the plough. Their temper's shorter than a winter's day. A cough's had them these two weeks.
  - Eliza [lord]: Eliza holds the manor's leases. They mean to be more than they were born. Last night's cup is still on them.
  - Bram [lord]: Bram holds the manor's leases. They're the first with a loaf when a house is bare. They never miss a service. Their purse strings are knotted tight. They buried one of their own last week.
  - Corvin [lord]: Corvin holds the manor's leases. They bow to no one. Their temper's shorter than a winter's day. They'd sooner watch the clouds than the plough. A cough's had them these two weeks.
  #0 Bram [Greet/Framing]: May the words said here do more good than harm. Amen.
  #1 Reeve [Question/Framing]: What ails the village most, friends? Speak true.
  #2 Bram [Remark/Answer] (re #1): We buried two souls this season; the churchyard grows crowded.
  #3 Corvin [Remark/Answer] (re #1): The ditch enclosing the common wood has been unlawfully fenced off.
  #4 Eliza [Remark/BuildOn] (re #3): Every cottager has a right to graze two cows on the village green.
  #5 Bram [PushBack/Object] (re #4): Talk is cheap - who will lift a hand to do aught about it?
  #6 Aldric [Remark/BuildOn] (re #4): If we lose the commons, our way of life is broken forever.
  #7 Reeve [Question/Framing] (re #1): Hold - we've wandered. Speak to the matter.
  #8 Corvin [Remark/Answer] (re #7): The common land belongs to the whole parish, not to a single estate.
  #9 Bram [Remark/BuildOn] (re #7): The rents increase every Michaelmas without mercy or reason.
  #10 Eliza [Remark/Answer] (re #1): I cannot pay in silver what the lord's steward demands.
  #11 Aldric [Farewell/Framing]: May what we spoke of take root. Amen, and good night.
