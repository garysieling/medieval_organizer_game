# Lit Review - Sprint 4: Community Organizing & Leadership Development

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Domain:** Community Psychology / Organizing
**Sources:** Transformation to leadership, youth organizing ecosystem, empowerment/power (Speer), organizing vs mobilizing (Han)

## Sources

### 1. Transformation to Leadership (J Comm Psych 2019)
- **Leadership development = learning about self, community, organization, system**
- **Potential for transformation** among community residents building collective power
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - leaders aren't born, they're developed through organizing

### 2. Youth Organizing Ecosystem (J Comm Psych 2022)
- **Leadership development ecosystem:** established leaders engage younger participants
- **Intergenerational:** older leaders support development of new ones
- **Relevance:** HIGH - organizing sustains via leadership pipeline

### 3. Community Organizing: Ecological Route to Empowerment (Speer 1995)
- **Reciprocal relationship:** development of power for orgs + individual empowerment for members
- **Empowerment processes/outcomes at multiple levels** (Zimmerman matrix)
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - org power and individual power co-develop

### 4. Organizing and Democracy (Annual Reviews 2024)
- **Two types:** organizing (transform individuals/groups into agents) vs mobilizing (aggregate preferences)
- **Organizing builds capacity, not just voice**
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - our game is organizing (building agents), not just mobilizing

## Key Mechanics to Extract

1. **Leadership development:** learning about self, community, org, system
2. **Transformation:** residents become agents through organizing
3. **Ecosystem:** established leaders develop new ones (intergenerational)
4. **Power co-develops:** org power + individual empowerment together
5. **Organizing != mobilizing:** building agents vs just aggregating preferences
6. **Multiple levels:** individual, org, community, system all affected

## Design Proposals

- **Leadership pipeline:** identify potential -> develop -> they lead -> develop others
- **Transformation:** citizens become agents through organizing work
- **Power co-development:** org grows stronger AND members feel empowered
- **Organizing as building:** not just protest; capacity construction
- **Multi-level effects:** individual (skills), org (capacity), community (power), system (change)

## Rating
**Critical** - Organizing is our core theme: leadership development, power building, transformation, multi-level effects. Validated that organizing != mobilizing - we're building agents, not just voices.
