# Fun Rubric - Draft for Fun Inspector

How we measure "fun" at human speed. Balance of grind + emotional stimulation.

## Core Principles
1. **Grind has a payoff** - effort must feel rewarded, not endless
2. **Emotional stimulation** - attachment to characters, stakes, surprise
3. **Emergent moments** - unexpected events that create stories
4. **Player agency** - decisions matter, not just watching

## Signals of Fun
- **Engagement peaks**: moments of tension, accomplishment, surprise
- **Pacing rhythm**: buildup → climax → breather (not constant grind)
- **Investment**: caring about villagers, their stories, outcomes
- **Discovery**: learning how systems interact, "aha" moments
- **Comeback potential**: struggling but with paths to recovery

## Signals of Grind (bad)
- **Repetitive actions** with no payoff or variation
- **Waiting** without anything interesting happening
- **Meaningless choices** (all paths lead to same outcome)
- **No stakes** - nothing to lose, no consequences
- **Stuck states** - no way forward or backward

## Fun Inspector Checks
Per playtest, score 1-5 on:
- [ ] Tension exists (something at risk)
- [ ] Progress is visible (I can see things changing)
- [ ] Surprise happened (something unexpected)
- [ ] I cared about an outcome
- [ ] I made a meaningful decision
- [ ] I want to keep playing (or watch what happens next)

## Red Flags (auto-flag for review)
- 10+ ticks with no state change
- All villagers idle simultaneously for 50+ ticks
- Hunger oscillates identically forever (no variation)
- No new events for 100+ ticks
- Player has <2 meaningful choices per session

## Notes for Playtesters
- Record moments of boredom AND excitement with tick numbers
- Flag "I would alt-tab here" moments
- Flag "tell me more about that" moments
- Rate each session: would you voluntarily play 10 more minutes?
