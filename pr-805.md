## Summary

- **Pandemic at scale (#805):** "the pestilence" joins the village's calendar of fevers — but unlike la grippe's scripted arrival, this one walks in off the roads on the carriers' own backs, settlement to settlement, and leaves a labor shortage the living feel in their wages.
- **`sim/illness.rs`:** `DiseaseKind::Plague` — 4-day silent incubation (a few days' walk before the bubo tells), `Route::Contact` so it spreads by proximity like any fever, no season (it keeps no calendar — it comes when the roads carry it), `immunity_gain` 0.05 (beating it teaches the body nearly nothing, so waves can revisit), no relapse.
- **The roads are the vector, not a site flag:** `country_tick` rolls rarely (~1/1400 days) on a dedicated `plague_rng` stream — whether the pestilence comes doesn't move the village's other draws — and `seed_plague` puts it on somebody who actually walks: a site `carrier` (cart drivers, provision riders) or a post `courier`. From there the existing machinery does the rest: the carrier sheds once incubation clears, site folk catch it at every stop, and those folk carry it onward on their own errands, season-work calls, and the next site's carts. The village gets seeded when a cart, rider, or season hand reaches the market — watchable, not probabilistic. No abstract site-level pandemic state, per the grill.
- **Mortality is plausible, not universal:** the seed's severity rolls in a band (1.12–1.29) straddling the model's kill threshold, so some years the pestilence burns through a town and some it only scares it — and the carrier's strength fades the farther down the road it walks, so the second settlement it reaches is a milder wave than the first. Empirically: a fierce wave fells about half a crowd or more, a fading one spares nearly all.
- **The aftermath lands in wages:** `Game::wage_mult()` counts the village's able hands (living, grown, non-outlander, not actively sick) against a 24-hand baseline and lifts every hired hand's day — `KID_DAY_WAGE` field work and odd-job pay — up to 3x as the dead and the burning take the rows. Sumptuary-law and feudal-obligation tails stay deferred per the grill.
- **Couriers are people now:** post riders get `Health` so the pestilence can ride them (and other fevers can too); `post_tick` skips the dead so a rider lost on the road isn't dispatched again.
