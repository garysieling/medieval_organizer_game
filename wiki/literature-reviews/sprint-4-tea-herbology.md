# Lit Review - Sprint 4: Tea History + Herbology/Apothecary

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Domain:** Economic History / Psychometrics / Herbal Medicine
**Sources:** tea trade (Industrial Revolution), Empire of Tea, Dutch physicians on herbal remedies

## Sources

### 1. Importing sobrie'tea' - Tea Trade (Industrial Revolution)
- **Caffeine = stimulant:** 14-61mg per serving, energizes/fights fatigue
- **Economic effect:** tea culture let workers work LONGER hours (real economic driver)
- **Civilizing agent:** acceptable stimulant (not inebriation/rowdiness like alcohol)
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - tea as psychometric effect (work endurance) + trade good

### 2. Empire of Tea (Ellis/Coulton/Mauger)
- **Global commodity:** first regular China<->Britain exchange, East India Company
- **Ubiquity:** cottages to castles - universal drink
- **Controversy:** Boston Tea Party, Opium Wars - imperial industry
- **Relevance:** HIGH - tea drove international trade + geopolitical conflict

### 3. British Way of Tea (1690-1730)
- **Knowledge production:** tea was exotic/luxury/sanative - Dutch physicians debated effects
- **Experimental encounter:** no language for flavor, creative culinary/scientific process
- **Relevance:** HIGH - tea as medicinal/knowledge object, not just drink

## Tea Mechanics

1. **Caffeine effect** - alertness boost, longer work hours (real economic impact)
2. **Trade good** - drove East India Company, international commerce, imperial conflict
3. **Ritual/social** - ceremony, sociability, knowledge production
4. **Varieties** - green/black/herbal/oolong/pu-erh, different caffeine + ritual value
5. **Civilizing** - acceptable stimulant (vs alcohol) -> social acceptance

## Herbology/Apothecary Mechanics

- **Witchy medicine tree:** chamomile (calm), willow bark (pain), yarrow (heal), lavender (salve), peppermint (energy), mugwort (vision), sage (purify)
- **Preparation matters:** tea/poultice/tincture/salve/smoke - different methods, different effects
- **Pseudo-medical:** pre-scientific remedies, apothecary knowledge, practitioner skill
- **Community care:** herbology as folk medicine, not professional

## Design Proposals

- **Tea as dual good:** trade commodity + consumable with psychometric effect (work endurance)
- **Psychometric stats:** caffeine alertness, ritual sociability - affect productivity + relationships
- **Herb tree:** progression from basic calming (chamomile) to advanced (mugwort/sage)
- **Apothecary:** NPC/career path, knowledge grows, remedies treat illness system
- **International trade:** tea + textiles as the luxury goods that link distant economies
- **Social rituals:** tea ceremony (sociability), salve healing (care), smoke vision (mysticism)

## Rating
**Critical** - Tea is both trade good AND psychometric consumable (work hours). Herbology is the medicine tree for illness system. Both are rich mechanics.
