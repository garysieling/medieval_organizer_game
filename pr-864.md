## Summary

- **IAF accountability assembly (#864):** the campaign gets a standing institution of its own — not another meeting, but a session on its own calendar that calls itself, publishes its agenda ahead, and holds the leader's yes/no answers to a deadline the village can check.
- **`game/campaign.rs`:** new `Assembly` component (cadence 14d, `announced`, published `agenda`, `last_turnout`, `musterered`, `held`, `pledges`) plus `Pledge { question, leader, answer, day, deadline, challenged, kept, check }` and `PledgeCheck` — five observable predicates (`Action`, `Meetings`, `Stronger`, `Delegation`, `Doubters`) that read world state at the deadline rather than tracking a promise.
- **The agenda is the accountability:** `announce_assembly` posts 3 pin-down questions picked deterministically (rotated by tick + campaign so assemblies don't ask the same three forever); the 2-day `PUBLISH_GAP` before `hold_assembly` sits is what makes the answers a record instead of an ambush.
- **Turnout reads the same ties as a meeting:** coordinator seat → shadow elite → leader drives; members at `ATTEND_MIN_TIE` answer. ≥3 attendees records `Transformational`, ≥2 `Transactional` — a thin house earns little.
- **Testimony amends:** in a real room a member's testimony can flip the leader's recorded answer before it's inked (`Pledge.challenged`); the challenge itself lands a low-grade `Conflict` interaction — challenges feed the same trust ledger as everything else.
- **The deadline keeps score:** `resolve_pledges` marks each pledge kept or broken (`kept = answer == observable_happened`). Broken words chill the room that answered (Conflict interactions musterered→leader, banked consent spent via `record_consent(false)` — the seam #853's watchdog reads through `broken_pledges()`); kept words warm it. `PLEDGE_CAP` drains the oldest resolved first.
- **Session wiring:** `assembly_tick` in the day loop establishes the institution ~10 days into the fight, calls on cadence, sits after the publish gap, resolves due pledges, and logs each beat. The organizer can call it early through a new `CtxAct::Assembly` verb (once a day, hidden while an agenda stands).
- **Wire:** `CampaignV.assembly` (`#[serde(default)]`) carries the standing — "assembly called - the agenda's posted", or the pledges' count ("2 pledges broke - 1 pledge stands") — appended to the client's campaign cadence line; `docs/protocol.md` updated.
