## Summary

- **Crisis cascades (#807):** famine → disease → unrest now chains through hooks the systems already have — no crisis tracker, no parallel unrest meter. A new `cascade` subsystem (`session/subsystems/cascade.rs`) runs a daily misery pass and a weekly census beside `land_tick`.
- **Hunger → immunity** was already halved in `IllnessSystem`; a session test now pins it (`test_hunger_halves_what_the_fever_clears`).
- **Bodies/flux → epidemic:** `land_tick`'s weekly water pass now (a) doubles effluent gain and waterborne-draw odds while `village_famine()` (≥2 in 5 living bellies under 40 hunger) holds, and (b) lets each unburied, non-outlander corpse foul the reach tile nearest where it fell (+0.01/census) — interred dead in `villager::Buried` stay out of it.
- **Misery → grievance:** a `desires::Hardship` streak component counts consecutive days at `Hunger < 40` or symptomatic sickness; past 5 days it lands `Problem::Hungry`/`Problem::Sick` (new variants with speech, labels, and issue tags), refreshed daily and lifted the day the want's met again — so the existing sanction ladder, campaigns and uprising organizers can spend them.
- **Story detection:** `sim/story.rs` gains `EventKind::Hardship`/`Unrest` and `StoryTheme::Calamity`, plus `detect_cascade` — the census writes only *crossings* (edges, not levels) into `Game::cascade_tale` as a 150-day working window, and when want→fever→(dead|anger) falls in order the village names the stretch once per 90-day cooldown: "the hunger year", "the pestilence", or "the great dying". Each domino also gets a log beat; no banner/ticker UI.
- `view_model.rs` biography/tale matches extended so the new kinds/themes render ("knew the empty pot", "felt the anger rise", "a tale of calamity").
