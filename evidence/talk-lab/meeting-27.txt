=== House meeting: Reeve convenes Aldric, Eliza, Bram, Corvin (seed 27) ===
  - Aldric [clergy]: Aldric tends the village's prayers. They'd give the coat off their back and call it lending. Work sits down beside them and they let it wait. Their temper's shorter than a winter's day. There's a wheeze in their step this season.
  - Eliza [lord]: Eliza holds the manor's leases. They mean to be more than they were born. Last night's cup is still on them.
  - Bram [lord]: Bram speaks for the manor hereabouts. They'd give the coat off their back and call it lending. The bell knows their step better than their own door does. A coin leaves their hand the way teeth leave a jaw. A fresh grave sits behind their eyes.
  - Corvin [lord]: Corvin's rents come due on the quarter days. They'd rather lose than ask. They burn fast and cool slow. They'd sooner watch the clouds than the plough. A cough's had them these two weeks.
  #0 Bram [Greet/Framing]: May the words said here do more good than harm. Amen.
  #1 Reeve [Question/Framing]: What ails the village most, friends? Speak true.
  #2 Bram [Remark/Answer] (re #1): We buried two souls this season; the churchyard grows crowded.
  #3 Corvin [Remark/Answer] (re #1): The common pasture is overgrazed; we must stint the number of sheep.
  #4 Eliza [Remark/BuildOn] (re #3): Every cottager has a right to graze two cows on the village green.
  #5 Bram [PushBack/Object] (re #4): Talk is cheap - who will lift a hand to do aught about it?
  #6 Aldric [Remark/BuildOn] (re #4): If we lose the commons, our way of life is broken forever.
  #7 Reeve [Question/Framing] (re #1): Hold - we've wandered. Speak to the matter.
  #8 Corvin [Remark/Answer] (re #7): The common land belongs to the whole parish, not to a single estate.
  #9 Bram [Remark/BuildOn] (re #7): The tolling bell reminds every family that life is fragile.
  #10 Aldric [Remark/BuildOn] (re #9): No family should be left alone to suffer when fever strikes.
  #11 Aldric [Farewell/Framing]: May what we spoke of take root. Amen, and good night.
