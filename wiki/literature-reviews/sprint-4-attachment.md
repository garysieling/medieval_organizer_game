# Lit Review - Sprint 4: Attachment & Relationship Psychology

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Domain:** Attachment Theory / Social Psychology
**Sources:** Petters (attachment modeling), simulated relationships, Shaver/Mikulincer (adult attachment)

## Sources

### 1. Petters - Attachment Modelling: From Observations to Designs (2017)
- **Attachment control system** reconceptualized as cognitive architecture for multi-agent sims
- **3-stage modeling:** survey attachment research -> behavioral scenarios -> simulation designs
- **Strange Situation + AAI:** behavioral measures -> emergent patterns
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - directly models attachment emergence in agents (what we did in psychology.rs)

### 2. "Choose Your Own Adventure" - Attachment Dynamics in Simulated Relationship (PSPB 2007)
- **Insecure individuals make destructive choices** in relationships, accumulate over time
- **Attachment styles predicted choices** in a simulated dating story
- **Relationship satisfaction linked to choices** even with same partner behavior
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - attachment styles drive behavior in simulated relationships (what we model)

### 3. Shaver/Mikulincer - Attachment Theory & Styles in Adulthood
- **Humans evolved attachment system** for survival/reproduction, needs protection/cooperation
- **History of close relationships shapes parameters** of attachment system
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - our attachment styles emerge from episode histories (anxiety/warmth)

### 4. Attachment in Adulthood (Annual Reviews)
- **Emotionally powerful experiences** from development/maintenance/disruption of attachment
- **Relevance:** HIGH - attachment relationships drive strong emotions in gameplay

## Key Mechanics to Extract

1. **Attachment styles emerge** from relationship history (anxious, avoidant, secure, disorganized)
2. **Styles drive behavior** - insecure individuals make destructive choices in relationships
3. **Styles accumulate** - repeated interactions compound over time
4. **Relationships are the strongest emotions** - attachment drives powerful experiences
5. **History shapes parameters** - past relationships affect how new ones form

## Design Proposals

- **Attachment styles emerge** from episode history (not assigned)
- **Styles drive choices** - anxious villagers seek reassurance, avoidant withdraw, secure connect
- **Relationship outcomes vary** - same interaction produces different results by style
- **Accumulation matters** - relationship quality builds (or degrades) over time
- **Emotional stakes** - attachment relationships are the most powerful gameplay

## Rating
**Critical** - Attachment psychology validates our emergence model: styles arise from history, drive behavior, and produce the strongest emotional gameplay. Petters' multi-agent sim approach matches ours.
