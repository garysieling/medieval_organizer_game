# Lit Review - Sprint 4: Emergence & Complex Adaptive Systems

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Domain:** Complexity Science / Emergence
**Sources:** AlChemy (Fontana/Buss), CAS methods, adaptive networks, Stigmergy Game

## Sources

### 1. AlChemy: Self-organization in computation (Fontana/Buss)
- **Simple rules -> complex stable organizations** in combinatorially large possibility space
- **Key finding:** complex organizations emerge MORE frequently than expected, robust against collapse
- **Mix of robustness + fragility:** stable orgs emerge but can't easily combine into higher-order entities
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - validates our design philosophy: small rules -> complex outcomes

### 2. Studying Complex Adaptive Systems (CAS methods)
- **CAS = many components that adapt/learn as they interact**
- **Signal-processing rules run in parallel** -> flexibility + breadth
- **Modularity:** groups of rules combine as "subroutines" -> building blocks for novel situations
- **Relevance:** HIGH - modularity = composable mechanics (exactly our approach)

### 3. Adaptive Coevolutionary Networks (PMC review)
- **State + topology co-evolve:** networks change AND node states change together
- **Complex topologies emerge from simple local rules** (epidemics, opinion formation)
- **Spontaneous emergence of different node classes** from homogeneous population
- **Relevance:** CRITICAL - our villagers + relationships co-evolve (state + network)

### 4. Co-Adaptation / Stigmergy Game
- **Agents adapt to environment changes induced by other agents**
- **Niches emerge:** only subset of environmental states appear with high probability
- **Wealth appears despite zero-sum start** - co-adaptation generates structure
- **Relevance:** HIGH - indirect interaction via environment produces emergent structure

## Key Insights

1. **Simple rules in large possibility space -> complex stable organizations** (exactly our philosophy)
2. **Emergence is more frequent + robust than expected** - not fragile
3. **Modularity = building blocks** - combine rules for novel situations
4. **State + topology co-evolve** - agents change AND their networks change
5. **Co-adaptation produces structure** - even in featureless environments, niches + wealth emerge

## Design Proposals

- **Simple local rules** - each system is simple; complexity emerges from interaction
- **Modular components** - rules combine as building blocks (not anticipated, emergent)
- **State + topology co-evolve** - villagers change AND their relationship network changes
- **Indirect interaction** - agents affect each other through environment (stigmergy)
- **Expect emergence** - complex organizations WILL arise, robustly

## Rating
**Critical** - This research directly validates the core design: small rules -> complex outcomes, emergence is expected + robust, modularity enables novel combinations, co-adaptation produces structure from nothing.
