=== House meeting: Reeve convenes Aldric, Eliza, Bram, Corvin (seed 27) ===
  - Aldric [clergy]: Aldric serves the village church. They're the first with a loaf when a house is bare. They'd sooner watch the clouds than the plough. Their temper's shorter than a winter's day. A cough's had them these two weeks.
  - Eliza [lord]: Eliza holds the manor's leases. They mean to be more than they were born. Last night's cup is still on them.
  - Bram [lord]: Bram holds the manor's leases. They're the first with a loaf when a house is bare. They never miss a service. Their purse strings are knotted tight. They buried one of their own last week.
  - Corvin [lord]: Corvin holds the manor's leases. They bow to no one. Their temper's shorter than a winter's day. They'd sooner watch the clouds than the plough. A cough's had them these two weeks.
  #0 Bram [Greet]: May the words said here do more good than harm. Amen.
  #1 Reeve [Question]: What ails the village most, friends? Speak true.
  #2 Bram [Remark] (re #1): We buried two souls this season; the churchyard grows crowded.
  #3 Corvin [Remark] (re #1): The ditch enclosing the common wood has been unlawfully fenced off.
  #4 Eliza [Remark] (re #3): Our ancient right of turbary and gathering brushwood must be defended.
  #5 Bram [PushBack] (re #4): Talk is cheap - who will lift a hand to do aught about it?
  #6 Aldric [PushBack] (re #3): And what wouldst thou have the village do, Corvin?
  #7 Corvin [Remark] (re #2): The herb-wife needs clean well water and willow bark for remedies.
  #8 Aldric [Agree] (re #4): Aye, there is the truth of it.
  #9 Bram [Remark] (re #6): The common land belongs to the whole parish, not to a single estate.
  #10 Aldric [Farewell]: Go in peace - and keep what was said here.
